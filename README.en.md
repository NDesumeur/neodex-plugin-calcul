# Mental Maths

Series of 10 quick sums against the clock, in harder and harder tiers. In pixel art.

*Version française : [README.md](README.md)*

## 2-minute sprint and daily challenge (new in 1.1.0)

In the level list, **2-minute sprint**: as many sums as you can in two minutes (additions and subtractions up to 200, times tables up to 12 × 100, divisions). Type the answer: as soon as it is right, the next one appears, no Enter. Your record and average are kept, along with the average time per operation. **Daily challenge**: the same sums for everyone, counted once a day with its streak.

## Controls

- **Keyboard**: type the result, **Enter** to confirm, **Backspace** to fix.
- Or pick one of the **4 answers**: **arrows** then **Enter**, or a click.
- **Gamepad**: d-pad or stick picks an answer, **A** confirms, **Start** pauses.
- **Full screen**: the button next to New series (Esc to come back). The slate takes all the room of the window, small or large.
- **P** or **Escape**: pause.

## Tiers

1. Additions · 2. Subtractions · 3. Up to 100 · 4. Times tables · 5. Divisions · 6. All four · 7. Big products · 8. Two operations · 9. Squares and percentages · 10. Expert

- **8 out of 10**: one star and the next tier. **9**: two stars. **10**: three.

## Data and sync

Tiers, stars and records are saved by NeoDex and synced between your PCs if sync is on.
